This is an archive of works that were originally posted on AO3 and saved by myself or found on the 'Phaidei' drive. It is simply a means to make navigating works easier instead of using google drive in this time where ao3 is down

If you are the author of any of these works and would like them removed, please contact kooguang on twitter and it will be removed! I tried to ask as many authors personally for permission but many didn't have their socials accessible or dms open

If you have a work (or multiple!) you want added to the archive you can also contact me!

After a lot of websites have been called out for trying to monetize fics / using AI in the making of the website, I just want to underline this is merely a fun small personal project I worked on as a CS major with nothing too complicated and that simply reads saved epubs I input and stores the tags to make navigating fics easier! I just made this because I myself have been suffering from AO3 whitdrawals and needed to read Mydei Pussy. Yipee!
